١. عند مكتب تسجيل الدخول

Agent: Where are you flying today?

Passenger: I'm flying to Los Angeles.

Agent: May I have your tickets?

Passenger: Here you go.

Agent: Is anybody else traveling with you?

Passenger: No. It's just me.

Agent: Do you have your passport with you?

Passenger: Yes. Here it is.

Agent: Can you place your bag on the scale?

Passenger: Sure.

Agent: Would you like an aisle or a window seat?

Passenger: Window seat, please.

Agent: I am placing you in 21A. The gate number is C2. They will start boarding 20 minutes before the departure time.

Passenger: Great. Thank you.

٢. التفتيش الأمني

Officer: Please lay your bags flat on the conveyor belt, and put your phone and all electronic devices in the bin.

Passenger: Do I need to take my laptop out of the bag?

Officer: Yes, you do. Please take off your jacket, too.

Officer: Do you have anything in your pockets? Keys, cell phone, loose change?

Passenger: I don't think so.

Officer: Okay, come on through.

٣. الجمارك عند الوصول

Officer: May I see your passport, please?

Passenger: Sure. Here you go.

Officer: How long are you staying?

Passenger: Two weeks.

Officer: What is the purpose of your visit?

Passenger: I'm a tourist. Here for sightseeing.

Officer: Welcome to America.

مشاكل قد تواجهك

  • Your baggage is overweight. — وزن أمتعتك زائد.
  • Your carry-on luggage is too large. — حقيبة يدك كبيرة جداً.
  • Your flight is delayed. — رحلتك متأخرة.
  • Your flight has been cancelled. — أُلغيت رحلتك.
