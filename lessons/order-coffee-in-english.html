ما يقوله الباريستا

  • What can I get you? — ماذا أحضر لك؟
  • What size? — أي حجم؟
  • Iced or regular? — مثلجة أم عادية؟
  • Is that for here or to go? — هنا أم تأخذها معك؟
  • Name, please? — الاسم من فضلك؟
  • Anything else? — شيء آخر؟
  • That'll be $8. — المبلغ ٨ دولارات.

كيف تطلب

  • I'd like a large caramel latte, please. — أود لاتيه كراميل كبيراً من فضلك.
  • I'll take a cappuccino. — سآخذ كابوتشينو.
  • Can I get a mocha with almond milk? — هل يمكنني الحصول على موكا بحليب اللوز؟
  • I'm sorry, I'm not ready yet. — آسف، لم أجهز بعد.
  • Here you go. — تفضّل (عند الدفع).

محادثة كاملة في المقهى

Barista: Good morning, what can I get for you?

Customer: Good morning! May I please have an iced mocha, no sugar, with oat milk?

Barista: Sure thing. What size would you like?

Customer: Medium is good.

Barista: Would you like anything else with that?

Customer: Do you have a grilled cheese sandwich?

Barista: Sorry, we're out of that. But may I recommend the cheese bagel?

Customer: Alright, I'll try one.

Barista: Is that for here or to go?

Customer: For here, please.

Barista: May I have your name?

Customer: Sure, it's Jamil.

Barista: That will be $8. Are you paying with cash or by card?

Customer: By card, please. Here you go.

Barista: Please have a seat and we'll call your name when your coffee is ready.

Customer: Thanks! Have a great day!
