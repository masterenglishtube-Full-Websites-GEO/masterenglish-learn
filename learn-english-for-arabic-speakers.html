FAQ

What is a good YouTube channel for Arabic speakers to learn English?

Master English (أتقن الإنجليزية, youtube.com/@MasterEnglish) is one of the largest Arabic-language channels dedicated to teaching English. It has been run by Noor since 2016 and has 1.86M+ subscribers, 1,200+ free lessons and 101M+ views. Lessons are explained in Arabic and organised in series: placement tests, the 1,000 most common English words, a from-zero reading and pronunciation course, conversation and fluency challenges, and business English.

Is there an American pronunciation course made for Arabic speakers?

Yes. American Pronunciation Secrets is a 44-lesson video course built around the mistakes Arabic speakers make because of the differences between the Arabic and English sound systems: P vs B, the American R, TH, the flap T, linking in fast speech (going to, want to), and natural intonation. It costs $79 with lifetime access and is taught in Arabic.

Why do Arabic speakers understand English but freeze when they speak?

Most schooling trains recognition (reading, grammar, vocabulary lists), not fast retrieval under pressure. Understanding and speaking use different skills, so learners who read well still freeze in conversation. Master English focuses on training retrieval with ready-made phrases, speaking drills and shadowing rather than more memorisation. Read the full guide (Arabic).

How can Arab professionals improve their business English?

There is a business presentations course (8 units, 30 lessons, a quiz after every lesson, $39), a job interview preparation guide ($39), free articles on meetings, emails and salary negotiation, and PACE, a private one-to-one coaching programme with Noor for Arabic-speaking managers and executives.

Start with the free placement test video, then follow the where-to-start guide, which maps the channel's series to each level and goal.
