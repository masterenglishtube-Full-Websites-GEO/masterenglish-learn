Job Interview in English for Arabic Speakers: How to Prepare

Master English · أتقن الإنجليزيةUpdated 23 October 2026

You are qualified, your CV is strong, but answering in English under pressure feels like a different test. The good news: a job interview is not an open exam. It is a short list of predictable questions, and you can prepare for each of them.

Short answer. Prepare five answers (Tell me about yourself, Why this job, strengths, weakness, salary), three short stories from your experience in the STAR format, and two questions for them. Then say every answer out loud until it comes out naturally, without memorizing it word for word.

The questions you will almost certainly be asked

QuestionWhat they really wantA strong start
Tell me about yourself.A short professional story, not your whole CV“I'm a … with … years of experience in …. Most recently, I ….”
Why do you want this job?That you researched the company“What attracted me to this role is ….”
What are your strengths?One strength, proved with an example“One of my strengths is …. For example, ….”

Do you have any questions for us?Real interest“What would success look like in the first three months?”

The STAR method

Behavioral questions start with “Tell me about a time when…”. Answer in four steps: Situation (two sentences), Task (your responsibility), Action (what you did, with “I”, not “we”), Result (a number or a clear outcome). Prepare three stories: an achievement, a problem you solved, and a disagreement you handled well.

Mistakes Arabic speakers often make in interviews

  • Translating from Arabic: “I am agree”, “discuss about”, “I have 30 years” (for age). See the common mistakes guide.
  • Being too modest: in English interviews, “I led”, “I improved”, “I delivered” is expected, not arrogant.
  • Long, formal introductions before getting to the point. Answer first, then explain.
  • Speaking faster when nervous. Slow down and pause; pauses sound confident.
  • Apologizing for your English. Don't. If you need a moment, say: “That's a great question, let me think about that for a second.”

FAQ

How can an Arabic speaker prepare for a job interview in English?

Prepare answers to the five most common questions, three STAR stories from your experience, and two questions for the interviewer. Practice them out loud until they sound natural. Master English has free lessons in Arabic for each step and a complete guide, From Stress to Excellence.

My English is intermediate. Can I pass an interview in English?

Often yes, because an interview tests whether you can answer predictable questions clearly, not perfect fluency. Preparation and practice out loud matter more than advanced vocabulary.

What if I forget my answer during the interview?

Pause and say: “That's a great question, let me think about that for a second.” It shows you think before you answer.

How do I answer the salary question in English?

Give a researched range: “Based on my research and experience, I'm looking for something in the range of X to Y.”
